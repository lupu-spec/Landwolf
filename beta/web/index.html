Public property research

Use Property research to look up a street address or coordinates. These reference databases do not add sale listings or establish market value. Live MLS is not connected: MLS providers require data-access authorization. No paid API subscriptions are enabled.

All 50 states. Explicit coverage limits.

Federal feeds accept all 50 states, but may have no current records in a state. State and county inventories are partial. Arkansas county tax-delinquent auctions are connected; tax-lien certificate and nationwide pre-foreclosure feeds are not connected. IRS tax-seizure auctions are a separate sale type. A default notice does not establish an offer to sell or an auction date.
