# LandWolf

Platform: authenticated web application, FastAPI and TypeScript; plain HTML/CSS.
Top tasks: search land listings, inspect source records and deal assumptions.
Standing identity: supplied wolf logo, Romulus/Remus, white backgrounds and black/dark-grey text,
Light/Dark/Auto, existing dock and 44px primary controls.

## Beta scope

First pass: Explore heading, search surface, listing cards and map container.
Preserve every action, source/data disclosure, input, route, entitlement and calculation.
No production or staging promotion before all repository gates pass.

## Accepted exceptions

- Silver gradients are an existing owner-selected brand decision and covered by
  appearance tests. Keep the gradients; reduce repeated shadows and search blur.
- The dock is a familiar owner-selected navigation control. Preserve its geometry.
- Semantic source-health colors and labels remain; they convey real data freshness.
- The modal backdrop blur distinguishes an active dialog; do not treat it as a card.
- Auth headlines have an explicit wrapping regression contract; left unchanged in
  this narrow first pass. Broader copy cleanup remains a later beta iteration.

## Owner revision

The owner explicitly rejected the grey/glossy treatment. White appearance now
uses white page, header, cards and controls with neutral text and black active
controls. This supersedes earlier silver-gradient exceptions for Light mode.
Dark mode remains an explicit appearance option; the review preview starts Light.
The earlier immutable header and silver-theme pixel/color assertions require
a new approved appearance baseline; do not call those legacy assertions passed.
